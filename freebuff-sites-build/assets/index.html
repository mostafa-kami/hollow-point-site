02 Intel — the premise

The city went dark at 04:12.

District 11 · live tactical feed
Four operators · no relief coming

By dawn, only one crew was still answering.

A blackout rolls through the eastern districts and takes the grid with it. No comms, no streetlights, no way to tell a neighbour from a muzzle flash. Hollow Point is a close-quarters tactical shooter about moving through a city you can no longer read.

You lead Mara Voss and three other operators block by block, clearing stairwells and rooftops while the clock runs down. No respawns, no map markers — just sound, sightlines and the people beside you.
